Iman Khoraminezhad

From GPUs for genomics to generative AI

My PhD at the University of Michigan was about making GPUs easier to program and faster to run. I took that into genomics: Parabricks rebuilt genome analysis pipelines for GPUs, making them far faster and cheaper than the CPU tools labs were using.

NVIDIA acquired Parabricks in 2020. I led the team through the acquisition and grew it into NVIDIA's genomics platform, used by research labs and clinical teams around the world. Today I work on large language models and accelerated computing.

  1. 2014

    PhD, University of Michigan

    Research on efficient GPU programming.

  2. 2016

    Co-founded Parabricks

    CEO, from research prototype to acquisition.

  3. 2020

    Acquired by NVIDIA

    Led the team and scaled Parabricks inside NVIDIA.

  4. Now

    Generative AI at NVIDIA

    Large language models and accelerated computing.
