The Journey

From pioneering genomics on GPUs to the frontier of AI

After a PhD at the University of Michigan on efficient GPU programming, I co-founded Parabricks to revolutionize genomic analysis with high-performance GPU software — delivering dramatically higher throughput at a fraction of the cost of existing solutions.

In 2020, Parabricks was acquired by NVIDIA, where I led the team's integration and growth within NVIDIA's healthcare ecosystem. Today I work at the bleeding edge of Generative AI, exploring how large language models and accelerated computing are reshaping what's possible.

  1. Chapter 1 · 2014

    PhD, University of Michigan

    Doctoral research on efficient GPU programming: making massively parallel hardware easier to program and faster to run.

  2. Chapter 2 · Founder

    Co-founded Parabricks

    Set out to revolutionize genomic analysis with GPU-accelerated software, leading the company as CEO from research to acquisition.

  3. Chapter 3 · 2020

    Acquired by NVIDIA

    I led the team through the acquisition and scaled it inside NVIDIA, turning a startup's software into NVIDIA's flagship genomics platform, now accelerating sequencing analysis for research labs and clinical teams around the world.

  4. Chapter 4 · Today

    Generative AI at NVIDIA

    Working at the bleeding edge of large language models and accelerated computing, and sharing what I learn every week in Persian.
